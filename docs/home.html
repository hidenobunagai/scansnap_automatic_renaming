ScanSnap Automatic Renaming

A private Google Apps Script utility that gives scanned PDFs in Google Drive descriptive filenames, so they stay findable years later.

What this app does

ScanSnap scanners save files with generic names such as 2026-10-18_0001.pdf. This script runs on a timer inside one Google Account and turns those into names that describe the document, for example 2026-04-20_市役所_住民税_納付書.pdf.

  1. It watches a single folder in Google Drive where the scanner deposits new PDFs.
  2. For each new PDF it runs OCR through Google Drive and reads the extracted text.
  3. It sends the document contents to the Google Gemini API, together with a prompt that asks for a filename consisting of date, issuer, document type, and a short topic.
  4. It renames the file, copies it into an archive folder, and appends a row to a Google Sheets log recording what was renamed and why.

Who uses it

This is a personal, single-user tool. The only person who uses it is its owner, through the same Google Account that installed it. It is not a commercial product, not a public service, and it has no other users, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data it handles

The script works only with the Google Account that authorized it. It reads the PDFs in the ScanSnap folder, and it writes the renamed copies and the log inside that same account. Document contents are sent to the Google Gemini API to produce a filename; nothing is sent to any other third party. The full detail is in the privacy policy below.

Because this app is used by fewer than 100 users and only by its owner, it is not submitted for Google OAuth verification. Google therefore shows an “unverified app” warning during sign-in, which the owner acknowledges when authorizing the script.
